# Store icons

Most icons in icons/ come from the AppStream metadata of the packages they show (Alpine's
appstream.alpinelinux.org, or Flathub for a few packs), under those projects' licences.

icons/debian.png, the Debian pack's icon, is the Debian Open Use Logo
(https://www.debian.org/logos/openlogo-nd.svg), rendered at 64×64. Copyright (c) 1999
Software in the Public Interest, Inc. Released under the Debian Open Use Logo License
(https://www.debian.org/logos/#open-use), which permits its use to refer to Debian.
